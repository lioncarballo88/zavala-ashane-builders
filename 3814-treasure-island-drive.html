The Residence

Designed to feel refined, warm, and easy to live in.

3814 Treasure Island Drive is a new single-family residence by Zavala Ashane Builders in Walden, Montgomery, Texas. The one-story plan brings together an open kitchen, dining and family room, a private primary suite, two secondary bedrooms, a dedicated home office and covered outdoor living.

Every selection is coordinated around a modern-warm palette: soft white walls, Shaker cabinetry, white quartz with subtle gray veining, natural-oak-tone flooring, stainless-steel appliances and matte-black accents.
